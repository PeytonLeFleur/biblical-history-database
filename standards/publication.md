# Standard: Publication

Source: SPEC Part 16. Applied by Rule 05.

| Level | Criteria | Label shown |
|---|---|---|
| `preliminary` | Valid biblical reference. The summary matches the text. The literary classification is correct. External claims have traceable citations. Uncertainty is displayed. Automated checks pass. No minimum source count. | "Preliminary research" |
| `researched` | All five stages complete. Required searches performed. Accessible evidence evaluated. Important claims at `claim_checked`. Competing positions represented. Chronology and geography reviewed. Cross-references validated. Audit passes. | "Researched — not human reviewed" |
| `human_reviewed` | Explicit owner approval of a specific revision (decision record with date and version). | "Reviewed by project owner on DATE" |

- `researched` does not guarantee historical correctness, and the website says so.
- Human approval is never inferred.
- Every published record shows its research status, its last-reviewed date (or "not reviewed"), open disputes and research gaps.

## Completeness measures (SPEC 16.4)
These are tracked separately for each book: verse coverage, event extraction, historical research, external evidence, chronology validation, cross-referencing, source verification, human review and publication readiness. A book is never called "researched" because its verse extraction is complete.

## Public versus private
GitHub Pages serves static files only. Review controls, private review notes (`research/reviews/private/`), restricted downloads and fixtures are never exported.
