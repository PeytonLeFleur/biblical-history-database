# Standard: Theological Interpretation

Source: SPEC Part 5.

## Priority order for interpretation
1. The biblical text
2. Its immediate context
3. Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek where relevant
4. Historical and cultural background
5. Scripture cross-referencing Scripture
6. **Enduring Word** (David Guzik), the first preferred commentary
7. Other theological commentary
8. Independent historical and scientific evidence

The owner's worldview is Christian. The project still represents scholarly and interpretive disagreement accurately. Established academic positions are not suppressed because they differ from traditional conclusions, and scholarly consensus is not treated as infallible.

## Enduring Word use (SPEC 5.4)
- Consult it first for each relevant passage when it is accessible.
- Store the commentary title, passage, URL, author attribution (David Guzik), access date, a **concise original paraphrase**, and the interpretive claims drawn from it.
- Never mirror pages or bulk-scrape. Quotation limits follow `copyright.md`.
- If it cannot be accessed, record that limitation.
- Enduring Word is `layer: interpretation`. It is **never** independent historical corroboration.

## Prophecy
- Prophetic statements are `prophecy` records. Visions are `vision` records.
- A proposed fulfillment always names its interpretive approach (`preterist` | `futurist` | `historicist` | `idealist` | `mixed`), the theological framework where relevant (`dispensational`, `amillennial`, `postmillennial`, `historic_premillennial`, …), and its proponents.
- Disputed prophetic interpretation is never presented as established historical fact.

## Daniel (SPEC 5.5)
Represent both:
- the traditional sixth-century BC authorship and dating arguments, and
- the widespread critical position that favors substantial composition or final redaction in the second century BC (the Antiochus IV period).

Separate the scholarly arguments (linguistic, historical, textual) from the theological commitments on each side.

## Non-canonical texts (SPEC 5.6)
Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, and the Prayer of Azariah are excluded from the canonical Daniel inventory. Josephus, 1 Maccabees and other ancient literature may serve as **historical sources** (`ancient_primary_text`). They are never presented as Scripture.
