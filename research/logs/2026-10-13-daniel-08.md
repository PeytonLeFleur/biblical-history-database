# Session log: 2026-10-13, Daniel 8 (Milestone 5, session 8)

**Unit:** Daniel chapter 8 (27 verses; Hebrew resumes at 8:1). All verses reviewed; 10 candidates.

## Separation of layers
- **Event (the experience):** "Daniel's vision of the ram and the goat at Susa" (8:1–2, 15–19, 27).
- **Vision record:** 7 symbols.
  - Unlike ch. 7, **the text itself identifies** the ram as "the kings of Media and Persia" (8:20), the goat as "the king of Greece", the great horn as "the first king" (8:21), and the four horns as four kingdoms (8:22).
  - These are recorded as text-level identifications, linked to the Media, Persia and Macedonian-empire records by `direct_reference` relationships (layer: text).
  - The text does **not** name Alexander, the successors or the little horn.
- **Prophecy records (3), with 7 attributed interpretations:**
  1. The ram, the goat and the four kingdoms: Alexander and the successors (the lists differ between Jerome and Guzik).
  2. The little horn / the king of fierce face:
     - Antiochus IV (Josephus, Jerome, Driver: "admitted on all hands", Montgomery, Guzik)
     - Antiochus as a type of the Antichrist (dual fulfillment; not a fulfillment claim)
     - Rome (Newton)
  3. 2,300 evenings and mornings:
     - 1,150 days (Driver: "so most commentators")
     - 2,300 days (Jerome; Guzik, 171–165)
     - 2,300 years, 457 BC – AD 1843 (Miller 1842, read directly)
- **Identifications:** made only through 4 sourced `proposed_fulfillment` relationships, all queued for review:
  - the great horn → Alexander
  - the little horn → Antiochus IV
  - the little horn → Rome
  - the 2,300 → the rededication event

## Chronology
- **Dan 8 vision:**
  - After the Dan 7 vision (text: "after that which appeared to me at the first").
  - Before Belshazzar's death (operator inference).
  - Regency model only: 2–3 years after the entrusting, computed Jan 551 – Dec 549 BC.
  - Undated otherwise.
- **New external event: the rededication of the Temple** (25 Chislev 148 SE), anchored by a synchronism.
  - The window runs mid-Nov 165 – mid-Jan 163 BC and deliberately spans both Seleucid-era epochs.
  - Driver and Montgomery give **Dec 165** (Macedonian, autumn-312 epoch).
  - **Josephus's "154th Olympiad"** points to 164/163 by my own calculation (Ol. 1 = 776 BC). Many modern works give Dec 164; not verified directly.
  - Recorded as disputed and queued for review.
  - Its textual reference is John 10:22 (the Feast of the Dedication). No Daniel passage is used as a "mention", so the symbol is not merged with the event.
