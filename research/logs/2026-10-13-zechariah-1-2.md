**Visions**, M5 layering (vision record plus prophecy records):

**Prophecies:**
- **The horns cast down:**
  - Daniel's four empires: Keil, after Jerome, Abarbanel and Hengstenberg; Guzik.
  - All the hostile powers ("four" for completeness): Mitchell; Rashi as reported by Mitchell.
  - Neither reading proposes a fulfillment event, so no review is required.
- **Jerusalem without walls; flee Babylon; many nations joined to the LORD:**
  - Partial Persian-era fulfillment and ultimate Messianic fulfillment (Guzik). Proposed fulfillment; review.

- Keil, after Vitringa and Hengstenberg: "completed twice".
- Mitchell: counted from 605, or loosely.
- Recorded as not empirically testable.
