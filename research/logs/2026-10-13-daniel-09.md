# Session log: 2026-10-13, Daniel 9 (Milestone 5, session 9)

**Unit:** Daniel chapter 9 (27 verses). All verses reviewed; 8 candidates.

## Separation of layers
- **Events (the narrated experience):**
  - "Daniel reads Jeremiah's seventy years and prays" (9:1–19)
  - "Gabriel comes at the evening offering with the word of the seventy weeks" (9:20–27)
- **Prophecy records:**
  - **The seventy weeks (9:24–27).** The text gives 7 + 62 + 1 weeks and the events attached to them. It defines no "week" and names no decree, anointed one, prince or covenant-maker. 13 attributed interpretations, 12 of them proposed fulfillments.
  - **Jeremiah's seventy years** (Jer 25:11–12; 29:10). The later biblical rereadings in 2 Chr 36, Zech 1:12 and Dan 9 are recorded as text. 3 attributed interpretations.
- **Identifications and starting points:** only through sourced relationships, all queued for review:
  - 6 proposed identifications: Cyrus, Onias III, Antiochus IV, Rome, the triumphal entry, the crucifixion
  - 3 proposed starting points: Cyrus, Ezra 7, Nehemiah 2
  - 3 proposed end points of Jeremiah's seventy years: the fall of Babylon, Cyrus's decree, the Temple's completion

## Interpretive models documented
1. **Maccabean** (Driver, Montgomery):
   - 587 → Cyrus 538 → Onias III c. 171 → 164.
   - The 67-year excess is acknowledged and explained as ancient miscomputation (Schürer's parallels from Demetrius and Josephus, via Driver).
2. **Maccabean variant** (Hilgenfeld, Behrmann, via Driver): overlapping weeks from 605.
3. **Messianic continuous** (Newton, Miller; Auberlen and Pusey via Driver): from Ezra 7 (458/457).
   - Driver's five objections are recorded.
4. **Messianic with a gap / dispensational** (Anderson, read directly; Guzik):
   - 445 BC → 6 April AD 32, using 360-day years.
   - Objections recorded: AD 32 is contested (Guzik himself); the weeks should be consecutive (Driver); P&D's 1 Nisan differs.
5. **Jerome's catalogue**, each recorded as reported by Jerome:
   - Africanus
   - Eusebius (two schemes, plus the "seventy-year week")
   - Hippolytus
   - Apollinaris (futurist; not a fulfillment claim)
   - Clement
   - Tertullian
   - "the Hebrews"
   - Origen is noted in the description; he offered no scheme.

## Candidate decrees (each its own event, dated through new reign records)
- **Cyrus** (Ezra 1): his first Babylonian year → 538–537.
- **Darius I's confirmation** (Ezra 6): between his 2nd and 6th years → 521–515.
- **Temple completed**, 3 Adar of Darius's 6th year → 516–515 (spring 515 by P&D's table).
- **Artaxerxes' 7th year** (Ezra 7) → 459–457. P&D: 1 Nisan = 8 Apr 458; 1 Ab = 4 Aug 458.
- **Artaxerxes' 20th year** (Neh 2) → 446–444. P&D: 1 Nisan = 13 Apr 445.
  - The solver's conservative Nisan window (1 Mar – 1 May) cannot exclude an accession before 1 Nisan 465, so the computed range is wider than the P&D table. Engine limitation, recorded here.
  - A Tishri count from Neh 1:1 (giving 444) is recorded as a competing reconstruction.
- **New reigns:**
  - Artaxerxes I: accession window 25 Mar 465 – 3 Jan 464, from Cowley 6 ("year 21 [of Xerxes], accession year of Artaxerxes"); end 24 Dec 424 – 26 Feb 423.
  - Darius I: 29 Sep 522 – 4 Feb 521 to 30 Sep – 1 Dec 486.

## Chronology of Daniel 9
- **Prayer:** after Darius receives the kingdom (text), and 0–2 years after it (operator inference; **no identification of Darius assumed**). Computed: 539–536 BC.
- **Gabriel's arrival:** same day (text).
- After the Dan 8 vision (Gabriel "whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning").
