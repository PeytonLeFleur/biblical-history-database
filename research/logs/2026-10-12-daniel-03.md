# Session log: 2026-10-12, Daniel 3 (Milestone 5, session 3)

**Unit:** Daniel chapter 3 (30 English verses). All verses reviewed.

## Rule 01
- **Candidates:**
  - the image and its dedication (3:1–7)
  - the accusation and refusal (3:8–18)
  - the furnace (3:19–27)
  - the decree (3:28–30)
- These became three events, following SPEC 6.2: the dedication as parent, the refusal and furnace as one child, and the decree as a child.
- **Verse notes:**
  - 3:23: Greek Daniel inserts the Prayer of Azariah and the Song of the Three. They are non-canonical and indexed as a document (SPEC 5.6).
  - 3:30: the Aramaic text continues with 3:31–33 (English 4:1–3).

## Records
- **Place:** Plain of Dura, with two location hypotheses and **no coordinates** (Oppert's Duair mound; near Tekrit). The note that "Dura" may be a common noun is kept.
- **Chronology model:** "Septuagint (Greek) dates in Daniel". Brenton's 3:1 "in his eighteenth year" is modelled only there; it is absent from the Aramaic text and from the WEB Greek Daniel.
- **Document:** the Greek additions (WEB Greek Daniel 3:24–90).
