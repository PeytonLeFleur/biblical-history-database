# Session log: 2026-10-13 — Ezra 4–5 (Milestone 7, unit 4)

**Units:** Ezra 4 (24 verses) and Ezra 5 (17 verses). All 41 verses reviewed; 7 candidates.

## New events
- **Opposition from the people of the land; the Temple work ceases until Darius's second year** (4:1–5, 24). Computed 537–519 BC.
- **Accusations to Ahasuerus and the Rehum–Shimshai letter to Artaxerxes** (4:6–23). Ordered only after the foundation; which kings are meant is disputed, and no identification is imposed on the chronology.
- **Haggai and Zechariah; the work resumes** (5:1–2), in Darius I's year 2, 520–519 BC.
  - Parker & Dubberstein's table: 1 Nisan of year 2 is 3 Apr 520; month VI begins 29 Aug 520.
  - So Hag 1:1 is 29 Aug 520 and Hag 1:15 is 21 Sep 520 (historically plausible: it assumes Babylonian reckoning).
  - Later months of year 2 are illegible in the OCR, so they are given by month only.
- **Tattenai inquires and writes to Darius** (5:3–17). Computed 520–515 BC, before Darius's confirmation of the decree (M5 event).

## New entities
- Tattenai (attestation in a Babylonian document of 502 BC recorded as historically plausible; the tablet and Olmstead 1944 not read)
- Shethar-bozenai, Rehum, Shimshai
- Haggai, Zechariah son of Iddo

## Disputed (1 review)
**Which kings are meant in Ezra 4:6–23:**
- Xerxes and Artaxerxes I, the section placed by topic: Keil, with Kleinert and Schultz; Ryle, who thinks the compiler mistook the walls for the Temple; Guzik and Kidner.
- Cambyses and Pseudo-Smerdis: older expositors, as reported by Keil and Ryle; Josephus addresses the letter to "Cambyses".
- 1 Esdras 2 keeps "Artaxerxes" but places the letter before Darius and adds the Temple foundation.

## Recorded without harmonising
Ezra 5:16 and 1 Esd 6:20 credit **Sheshbazzar** with the foundation; Ezra 3:8–10 credits **Zerubbabel and Jeshua**. Both related questions are already under review.
