# Session log: 2026-10-13 — Nehemiah 7–8 (Milestone 7, unit 16)

**Units:** Nehemiah 7 (73 verses) and Nehemiah 8 (18 verses). All 91 verses reviewed; 4 candidates.

## Reused
- **The register (Neh 7:6–73)** is the existing event `return-of-exiles-with-zerubbabel-and-jeshua` (Ezra 2), which already carried Neh 7 as a parallel account. The new candidate is linked to it, with no revision bump.
  - Figures recorded as ambiguities (Keil): the separate entries sum to 31,089 (Neh) and 29,818 (Ezra) against 42,360. The gifts are 41,000 darics and 4,200 minas plus a defective item (Neh 7:70–72), against 61,000 darics and 5,000 minas (Ezra 2:69). Keil treats Ezra's figures as a textual error and round numbers.

## New records
### Event: Jerusalem guarded; the registry found (Neh 7:1–5)
- Hanani and Hananiah were put over Jerusalem, with rules for the gates and watches.
- The city was thinly populated (plausible, with the Neh 3 archaeology).

### Event: Ezra reads the Law on 1 Tishri (Neh 7:73b–8:12)
- Narrative-sequence date: 1 Tishri = 7 October 445 (P&D, Artaxerxes I year 20, month VII).
- **Disputed (review):** was it in Nehemiah's governorship, with both men present (Keil)? Or part of Ezra's own story, with "Nehemiah" in 8:9 added (Batten, following Meyer and Torrey)?
  - **1 Esdras 9:37–55, read directly:** follows the Ezra 10 parallel with this reading, and has "Attharates" in v. 49.

### Event: the Feast of Booths (Neh 8:13–18)
- 21–28 October 445 on the same reading.
- "Since the days of Joshua" (Keil): not that the feast had lapsed (Ezra 3:4), but that the whole community had not dwelt in booths.
