**Prophecies:**
- **The latter glory of this house** (Hag 2:6–9), 3 interpretations:
  - Messianic "Desire of all nations": the Vulgate and older commentators (as reported by Mitchell); Guzik. Proposed fulfillment; review.
  - The nations' precious things: Mitchell; Keil, for whom the Messianic reading is "foreign to the context".
  - An eschatological shaking: Hebrews 12:26–27; Guzik.
- **Zerubbabel the LORD's signet** (Hag 2:20–23), 2 interpretations:
  - Haggai expected Zerubbabel himself to be the Davidic king, a hope not realised: Mitchell.
  - A promise to David's line, fulfilled in the Messiah: Keil, after Koehler and Calvin; Guzik. Proposed fulfillment; review.
