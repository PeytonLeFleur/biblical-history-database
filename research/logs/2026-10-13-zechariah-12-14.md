**Disputed (review): authorship**

- A fourth, apocalyptic writer c. 217–204 BC (Mitchell).

### Cross-links to earlier research
- **14:5 → the Amos 1:1 earthquake in the days of Uzziah:** a direct text reference (historically plausible). Keil and Mitchell both identify it, and Josephus, *Ant.* 9.10.4, attaches an earthquake to Uzziah's intrusion into the temple. The earthquake evidence itself stays on the Amos event.
- **12:11 → Josiah's death at Megiddo (609):** the "mourning of Hadad-rimmon in the valley of Megiddo" (Targum, Jerome, Keil, Mitchell). Recorded at the interpretation layer, medium confidence. The Adonis/Tammuz view is rejected by both.
- **14:16–19 → the Feast of Booths of Neh 8:** a thematic link.
