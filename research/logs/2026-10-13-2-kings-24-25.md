# Session log: 2026-10-13 — 2 Kings 24–25 (Milestone 6, cross-book validation 1)

**Units:** 2 Kings 24 (20 verses) and 25 (30 verses). All verses reviewed; 10 candidates; 9 events.

## Events (reusing Daniel records where they exist)
- **Jehoiakim submits, then rebels** (24:1–4). Linked to the Dan 1:1 campaign as a thematic parallel. The possible identity is already under review on the Dan 1 record and is **not duplicated**. Dated after Carchemish (existing event).
- **Jehoiakim's death and Jehoiachin's reign** (24:5–9).
  - The manner of Jehoiakim's death is **disputed** (review): 2 Kgs 24:6; Jer 22:18–19; 2 Chr 36:6; Josephus *Ant.* 10.6.3, where he is killed and thrown before the walls.
- **Capture of Jerusalem and the deportation of 597** (24:10–16). Now the anchor of the existing **ABC 5 synchronism** (2 Adar, year 7 = 15/16 Mar 597). That synchronism had been left unanchored "pending Milestone 6".
  - The "eighth year" (2 Kgs 24:12) vs the "seventh year" (ABC 5; Jer 52:28) is recorded as a convention difference (Young 2004).
  - The deportee numbers differ: 10,000; 7,000 + 1,000; 3,023 (Jer 52:28); 3,000 (Josephus).
- **Zedekiah installed** (24:17–20). ABC 5 corroborates "a king of his own choice".
- **The siege begins** (25:1–2). Its start (Jan 589 vs Jan 588) is **disputed** (review).
- **Breach of the city and capture of Zedekiah** (25:3–7). **587 vs 586 is disputed** (review). Young (2004, read directly) argues 587; Thiele 586 (via Young); Guzik 586.
- **Burning of the Temple** (25:8–21). The day (7th, 10th or 1st of Ab) is **disputed** (review).
- **Gedaliah and his assassination** (25:22–26). Mizpah = Tell en-Nasbeh is probable (Pleiades: "less-certain").
- **Release of Jehoiachin** (25:27–30), early 561 BC. Amēl-Marduk's reign is taken from P&D; Josephus's 18 years is contradicted.

## Chronology (cross-book test)
- New reigns: Jehoiachin, Zedekiah, Amēl-Marduk (P&D windows).
- **Two models** for the fall of Jerusalem:
  - "586, accession-year count": Temple burned **Ab 586**.
  - "587, non-accession count in 2 Kings" (Young): Temple burned **Ab 587**. In this model, 2 Kgs 24:12's "eighth year" also contains March 597.
- Both solve without contradiction. Model-independent: 589–584 BC.
- Daniel's existing chronology is unchanged. Daniel's dates do not depend on the 587/586 choice.
