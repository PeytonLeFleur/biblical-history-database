# Session log: 2026-10-12, Daniel 4 (Milestone 5, session 4)

**Unit:** Daniel chapter 4 (37 English verses = Aramaic 3:31–4:34). All verses reviewed; versification notes recorded per verse.

## Rule 01
- **Candidates:**
  - the royal letter frame (4:1–3)
  - the dream and the summons of Daniel (4:4–9)
  - the dream content: the tree and the watcher (4:10–18), a vision child
  - Daniel's interpretation (4:19–27), a prophecy child
  - the boast and madness (4:28–33)
  - the restoration and confession (4:34–37)
- **Events:**
  - The tree dream and interpretation (4:4–27).
  - The boast, madness and restoration (4:28–37). This is a period event, and it also covers the letter frame.
  - Constraint: "at the end of twelve months" (4:29), 12 months after the dream.

## Records (global, reusable)
- **Person:** Nabonidus (aliases Nabû-naʾid and Nabunai in 4Q242).
- **Place:** Tayma (Pleiades 814739, verified point).
- **Artifact:** the East India House Inscription (BM 1938,0520.1; findspot Babylon).
- **Documents:** the Prayer of Nabonidus (4Q242) and ABC 7 (the Nabonidus Chronicle). ABC 7 will be reused for the fall of Babylon in Daniel 5.
- **Vision:** the great tree. Its in-text identifications (4:22, 26, 25) are kept separate from the symbols.
- **Prophecy:** the decree of the watchers. Two interpretations:
  - Fulfilled in the king's seven-year humbling (proposed fulfillment; **review queued**).
  - The source-critical Nabonidus view, which is *not* a fulfillment claim.
