# Session log: 2026-10-13 — Isaiah 44–45 (Milestone 6, unit 4)

**Units:** Isaiah 44 (28 verses) and Isaiah 45 (25 verses). All verses reviewed; 10 candidates.

## New records
- **Prophecy: Cyrus the LORD's shepherd and anointed** (Isa 44:24–45:13). Two interpretations, each a proposed fulfillment (queued):
  - **Predictive prophecy by Isaiah of Jerusalem, fulfilled 539–538.**
    - Josephus *Ant.* 11.1.1–2: Cyrus read his name in Isaiah.
    - Guzik, citing Poole and Motyer.
  - **An exilic prophet announcing Cyrus's imminent conquest, c. 546–539.**
    - Skinner 1898, after Driver, with Döderlein (1775) and Ibn Ezra cited.
    - Babylon's capture is still future in 45:2–3; Cyrus is already a famous conqueror.
  - Relationships:
    - Proposed fulfillment → the Cyrus decree (Ezra 1). Queued.
    - Proposed fulfillment → the fall of Babylon (539). Queued.
    - Thematic parallel → Cyrus Cylinder lines 12 and 15 (Marduk "took … by the hand", "called out his name").
    - Thematic parallel → Dan 9:25 "anointed one" (Driver identifies it with Cyrus).
    - Thematic parallel → Phil 2:10–11 (Isa 45:23).
