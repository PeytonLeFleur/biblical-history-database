## Chronology
- **Fall of Babylon:** Parker & Dubberstein (1942, p. 11) give VII/16/17 = **13 Oct 539**; the month began on 28 Sep.
  - Many modern works give 12 Oct. Wikipedia's citation for 12 Oct is a 2025 newspaper, so it was not relied on. The 12–13 Oct window records the convention difference.
  - Sippar fell on 11 Oct; Cyrus entered on 29 Oct.
  - A Uruk tablet dated by Nabonidus on 14 Oct shows the news had not yet reached the south.
- **Constraints:**
  - The feast falls within Nabonidus's reign.
  - Belshazzar's death is 0–1 days after the feast ("that night", text).
  - Belshazzar's death is 0–1 days after the fall. This is an **operator inference**, with the assumption stated, because Daniel does not date the night and no Babylonian source records his death.
  - Dan 1:21 falls in Cyrus's year 1 (Babylonian accession-year count; 24 Mar 538 – 12 Mar 537). The other counting is noted in the assumptions.
- **Computed windows (model-independent):**
  - fall: 12–13 Oct 539
  - feast: about 11–13 Oct 539
  - Belshazzar's death: 12–14 Oct 539
  - Dan 1:21: Cyrus's first year (engine's conservative Nisan windows)
