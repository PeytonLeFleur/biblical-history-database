## New records
### Event: Jerusalem repopulated, one in ten by lot (Neh 11)
- Known from Nehemiah only.
- Keil: this carries out the resolve of 7:4.

### Event: the priestly and Levitical registers to the days of Jaddua (Neh 12:1–26)
- **Johanan was high priest in 408/407** (strongly supported).
  - Cowley 30 names Johanan the high priest.
  - Josephus, *Ant.* 11.7.1, read directly: John son of Judas (Joiada) son of Eliashib.
- **Disputed (review):** is "Darius the Persian" (12:22) Darius II? Keil says yes: one enrolment while the four men lived. Josephus makes Jaddua high priest under Darius III and Alexander (*Ant.* 11.8.5). Cowley calls 12:22–23 a later addition.
- Genealogical links: Eliashib → Joiada → Johanan → Jaddua.
- Jaddua ↔ Alexander: a low-confidence link, cited to Josephus.

### Event: the dedication of the wall (Neh 12:27–13:3)
- Two thanksgiving companies (one led by Ezra, one with Nehemiah); the reading of Deut 23.
- Undated; Keil places it after chs. 8–11.

### Event: Nehemiah's return and second-term reforms (Neh 13:4–31)
- **Departure in Artaxerxes I's year 32 = 433/432** (plausible; Cowley). He returned before 424.
- Reforms: Tobiah expelled, Levites' portions restored, Sabbath trade stopped, mixed marriages confronted.
- **Disputed (review):** Joiada's son, son-in-law of Sanballat (13:28).
  - Keil and Cowley: the same as Josephus's Manasseh, misdated by Josephus.
  - Josephus, *Ant.* 11.7.2 and 11.8.2, read directly: places him under Darius III.
  - Older two-Sanballat view, as reported by Keil.
