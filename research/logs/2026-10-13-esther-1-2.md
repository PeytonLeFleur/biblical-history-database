### Event: Ahasuerus's third-year feast; Vashti deposed (Esth 1)
Critical priority; review queued.
- **Ahasuerus = Xerxes I** (historically plausible): Keil from the name and the empire's extent; the LXX and Josephus have Artaxerxes.
- **Strongly supported:** the council on the Greek expedition and four years of preparation (Herodotus 7.8, 7.20, read directly). Keil's comparison with the 180-day assembly is recorded as interpretation.

### Event: Esther made queen in Tebeth of year 7 (Esth 2:1–18)
Critical priority; review queued.
- **Date:** from 22 Dec 479 (P&D; historically plausible).
- **Disputed (review): historicity.** Keil defends it against Bleek and Bertheau (as he reports them).
  - Herodotus, read directly, names Xerxes's wife Amestris (7.61; 9.108–113) and nowhere mentions Vashti or Esther.
  - Guzik's statement (after Baldwin) that Herodotus mentions Hegai is **not borne out**: Herodotus's Hegias (9.33) is the brother of an Elean seer.
- Mordecai's age problem (2:6) recorded per Keil.
