# Session log: 2026-10-13 — Nehemiah 9–10 (Milestone 7, unit 17)

**Units:** Nehemiah 9 (38 verses) and Nehemiah 10 (39 verses; English versification, Hebrew 10:1 = English 9:38). All 77 verses reviewed; 3 candidates.

## New records
### Event: the fast of the twenty-fourth day and the Levites' confession (Neh 9)
- 24 Tishri, two days after the eighth-day assembly (Keil).
  - 30 October 445 on the narrative-sequence reading.
  - This depends on the disputed setting of Neh 8.
- The confession resembles Psalm 106 (Keil).
- "Servants" to foreign kings, read as Persian tribute (Keil; Neh 5:4; Ezra 4:13; 7:24): historically plausible, inferred from biblical texts only.
- Batten treats the chapter as an independent fast-day account; he calls the Greek "and Ezra said" (9:6) a late interpolation.

### Event: the sealed covenant (Neh 9:38–10:39)
- Signatories are led by Nehemiah. The pledges cover:
  - marriage
  - Sabbath trade
  - the seventh year and debts
  - one-third shekel: Keil reads it as the half-shekel of Exod 30:13 lowered for poverty
  - the wood offering by lot
  - firstfruits and tithes
- **Disputed (review):** sealed at the assembly of 24 Tishri (Keil), or a sequel to the second-term reforms of ch. 13 (Batten: four pledges match those reforms, and the author is a lay participant).
