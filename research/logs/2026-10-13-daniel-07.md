# Session log: 2026-10-13, Daniel 7 (Milestone 5, session 7)

**Unit:** Daniel chapter 7 (28 verses; the last chapter of the Aramaic section). All verses reviewed.

## Separation of layers
- **Event (the narrated experience):** "Daniel's night vision of the four beasts and its interpretation" (7:1, 15–16, 28).
- **Vision record (symbols):** 11 symbols. Each carries **only the identification the text itself makes**: four kings/kingdoms (7:17, 23), ten kings (7:24), and another king for the little horn (7:24–25). The text names none of them. The Ancient of Days and the son of man are not explicitly identified.
- **Prophecy records (4), with the interpretations attributed:**
  1. The four kingdoms:
     - Roman scheme
     - Grecian scheme
     - Porphyry's variant
  2. The ten horns and the little horn, with "time, times and half a time":
     - Antiochus IV
     - a future Antichrist
     - the papacy (historicist)
     - Calvin's Caesars, as reported by Guzik
  3. One like a son of man:
     - Jesus (Christian)
     - an individual Messiah (Second Temple and rabbinic)
     - a collective symbol of the saints
     - an angelic leader (Michael, Collins via Wikipedia)
  4. The heavenly judgment and the kingdom of the saints:
     - final judgment (Jerome amillennial vs Guzik premillennial)
     - God's judgment on Antiochus (Driver)
     - the papal dominion removed by degrees (Newton)
     - reception history (Enoch's "Head of Days"; Rev 1:14)
- **Identification relationships:** each kingdom or person is linked to a prophecy record only through a separately sourced `proposed_fulfillment` relationship, and every one is queued for owner review:
  - the first beast → Babylon
  - Media, Persia, Macedon, Rome
  - the little horn → Antiochus IV
  - the ten horns → the Seleucids
- Every interpretation records its proponents, textual reasoning, objections and acceptance where verifiable. Disputed identifications are never classified as fulfilled.

## "First year of Belshazzar"
- **Verified:**
  - The Verse Account (ii 5–6, Livius): the kingship was entrusted "when the third year was about to begin".
  - ABC 7: year 3 has the western campaign (Abu–Kislimu); years 7, 9, 10 and 11 have the king in Tayma and "the prince" in Akkad. Years 4–5 are lost.
  - Smith's correlation of the two, via Dougherty (pp. 105–107).
  - Parker & Dubberstein: year 2 began 19 Apr 554, year 3 on 7 Apr 553, year 4 on 26 Apr 552.
- **No Babylonian document counts years of Belshazzar.**
- **Model-independent:** the vision is undated. The only bound is that it comes before Belshazzar's death (an operator inference, stated as an assumption).
- **New chronology model "Belshazzar's years counted from the entrusting of the kingship":**
  - A regency record (coregent with Nabonidus; explicitly not a reign).
  - Start window 10 Jan – 28 Dec 553. The breadth of the window is a stated modelling assumption.
  - The vision falls 0–1 years after the start, covering both counting conventions. Computed: c. Jan 553 – Dec 551 BC.
  - The model is reusable for Dan 8:1 ("third year").
