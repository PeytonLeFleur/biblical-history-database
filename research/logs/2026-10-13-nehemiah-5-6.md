### Event: the wall completed on 25 Elul after fifty-two days (Neh 6:15–19)
Critical priority; review queued.
- **Date:** Parker & Dubberstein, Artaxerxes I year 20: Elul began 8 September 445, so 25 Elul = 2 October 445; the work began about 12 August (Keil: 3 Ab). Historically plausible.
- **Duration, disputed (review):** fifty-two days (MT; Keil with Bertheau) vs two years and four months (Josephus, *Ant.* 11.5.8; Ewald's emendation as reported by Keil). Batten: earlier work may have left little to build.
