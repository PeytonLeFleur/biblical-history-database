# Session log: 2026-10-13 — Ezra 2–3 (Milestone 7, unit 3)

**Units:** Ezra 2 (70 verses) and Ezra 3 (13 verses). All 83 verses reviewed; 9 candidates.

## New records
**Events:**
- **The return of the exiles with Zerubbabel and Jeshua; the list of returnees.** Computed 538–531 BC, after the Cyrus decree. Linked:
  - same event (high) with Neh 7:6–73
  - same event (medium, review) with Sheshbazzar's return (Ezra 1:5–11)
- **The altar rebuilt and the Feast of Booths kept** (538–530 BC). Constrained to precede the foundation (Ezra 3:6).
- **The foundation of the Second Temple laid.** Critical priority; 537–530 BC within Ezra's framework. The alternative date of 520 is recorded as disputed, not modelled.

**Entities:**
- Zerubbabel. His possible identity with Sheshbazzar stays under the existing M6 review and is not assumed.
- Jeshua (Joshua) son of Jozadak.
- Joppa (Pleiades 687931, certain).

## Findings
**List arithmetic** (strongly supported):
- The itemised figures fall short of the stated 42,360 in every version:

  | Version | Items | Sum |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ezra 2 | 42 | 29,818 |
  | Nehemiah 7 | 40 | 31,089 |
  | 1 Esdras 5 | — | 30,143 |

  The Ezra and Nehemiah sums were recomputed from the WEB text; the 1 Esdras sum was not, because of variant readings.
- The 1 Esdras total comes from Keil (and Kidner via Guzik).
- Josephus gives 42,462.

**Why the totals fall short** (disputed, review):
- Keil: clerical errors.
- The northern tribes are included in the total (Seder Olam, Rashi, Ussher, as reported by Keil).
- Ages 12–20 are counted (J. D. Michaelis, from 1 Esd 5:41).
- Batten: the Chronicler's large numbers.

**Date of the return** (disputed, review):
- Under Cyrus: Ezra; Keil.
- Under Darius I: 1 Esdras 5:1–6 and Josephus; Batten (later features). Ryle suggests a second contingent under Zerubbabel.

**Date of the foundation** (disputed, review):
- c. 536/535: Keil (535/534) and Ryle (536), who answer the objections from Ezra 5:2, Hag 2:18 and Ezra 5:16.
- 520: Batten; the scene is a displaced parallel.
- Under Darius: 1 Esdras and Josephus.
- Unhistorical: Kosters, as reported by Batten and Ryle.

**Darics** (historically plausible):
- Persian gold coins (Ryle); the derivation from Darius is "far from certain".
- Daric or drachma (Batten); Smend held the term a later introduction.
- Numismatics not researched.
