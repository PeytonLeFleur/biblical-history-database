# Session log: 2026-10-13 — Nehemiah 1–2 (Milestone 7, unit 13)

**Units:** Nehemiah 1 (11 verses) and Nehemiah 2 (20 verses). All 31 verses reviewed; 5 candidates.

## Completed M5 record
**"Artaxerxes commissions Nehemiah (Neh 2)"** (revision 2; no reviews depended on it).
- New claim (strongly supported): the king was Artaxerxes I, so the commission falls in 445.
  - Cowley: papyrus 30 shows Bigvai as governor in 408, so Neh 13:6's 32nd year must be Artaxerxes I, i.e. 433, "a fixed point".
  - Keil agrees.
  - Josephus (Xerxes, 25th year) is recorded as counter-evidence.
- Context: Anderson's "March 14, 445" (Guzik) vs Parker & Dubberstein's 13 April 445.

## New records
**Event: Hanani's report and Nehemiah's prayer** (Chislev).
- Competing explanations of "Chislev of the twentieth year" coming before Nisan of the same year:
  - an autumn year (Keil; Wellhausen via Batten);
  - an error for the nineteenth year (Batten);
  - Ryle's objections to the autumn year.
- Both explanations put the report in Chislev 446 (historically plausible).

**Event: arrival, the night inspection and the call to build** (2:9–20).
- **Elephantine evidence, read directly in Cowley's edition of papyrus 30:**
  - line 18: "Johanan the high priest"
  - line 29: "Delaiah and Shelemiah the sons of Sanballat governor of Samaria"
  - line 30: "20th of Marheshwan, 17th year of Darius"
  - The papyrus's existence and wording are independently corroborated. Identifying Nehemiah's Sanballat with the papyrus's (Cowley) is historically plausible.

**Entities:**
- People: Nehemiah, Hanani, Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem, and Johanan the high priest.
- Document: Elephantine papyrus Cowley 30.
- Cowley finds no basis for identifying the Passover letter's Hananiah with Nehemiah's Hanani; recorded on Hanani.
